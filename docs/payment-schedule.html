3. How the split relates to the work

Worth setting out, because the instalments are not proportional to the hours behind them — and the difference runs in the school's favour on the payment it makes first.

Of the 146 hours quoted, 80 are already built and running — 55% of the project. At the project rate of ~₹308 per hour, that delivered work is worth about ₹24,650.

The first invoice is ₹18,000. The remaining ₹6,650 of already-delivered work is carried into the two later stages:

4. Acceptance

So that "accepted" is not something to argue about later:

  1. Each stage is demonstrated to the school's point of contact, on the live system.
  2. The school has five working days to raise anything that does not work as described in Section 3 of the proposal.
  3. Anything raised in that window is fixed before the stage is accepted, at no extra charge.
  4. If nothing is raised within five working days, the stage is treated as accepted and the invoice is issued.

Point 3 is not limited to the five days — the 60-day warranty in Section 10 of the proposal continues to apply after handover.

5. Terms
