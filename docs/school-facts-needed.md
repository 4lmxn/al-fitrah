# What we need from the school

Every answer below unlocks a section that is already built and waiting on the
website. Until an answer arrives, that section simply does not appear — the site
never shows a placeholder, a "coming soon", or a number we guessed.

Answers go into one file: `src/content/facts.ts`. Nothing else has to change.

**Why this matters.** Parents choosing a preschool in Bangalore decide on
specifics: ratio, fees, safety, who the teachers are, and what the first two
weeks look like. Our site currently answers none of those. It says we are warm,
nurturing and caring — which is exactly what every competing site says, so it
persuades nobody. Each answer below turns one adjective into a fact.

Ordered by how much each one is costing us.

---

## 1. Fees — the single biggest gap

Right now the site says "the admissions team will share the full schedule during
enrollment". A parent who cannot find a price does not enquire to discover it.
They close the tab.

- What does a family pay for one year of Pre-KG, all-in?
- Is it the same for Junior KG and Senior KG? If not, what are those?
- Which one-time charges exist (admission fee, deposit, materials, uniform)?
- Can it be paid in instalments? How many, and on what dates?
- Sibling discount or need-based concession — what are the actual terms?
- What is **not** included in the number above?

## 2. Teacher-to-child ratio and class size

A top-three factor in every parent survey, and currently the loudest silence on
the site.

- How many children per teacher in each level?
- Maximum class size?
- Is there a helper or assistant in the room as well?

## 3. Safety, hygiene and pickup

The campus is on a third floor. That makes these questions more pressing to a
parent, not less — so answering them plainly is an advantage, not a risk.

- Who is allowed to collect a child, and how is that verified?
- What happens if someone else comes to collect?
- Is there CCTV? Where?
- What is the fire or emergency evacuation procedure from the third floor?
- How often are classrooms, toys and washrooms cleaned, and by whom?
- Is there a staff member trained in paediatric first aid?

## 4. Who the teachers are

The About page currently says "Guided by experts", which is a claim, not
evidence. Two or three named teachers with real qualifications would do more for
us than the entire rest of that page.

For each teacher we can name (with their permission):

- Full name and role
- Qualification (degree, Montessori/ECCE certification, Noor-ul-Bayan training)
- Years of experience with this age group

## 5. The daily timetable, with clock times

"Morning arrival and circle time" tells a parent nothing they could not have
guessed. A real timetable tells them what their child's morning looks like.

- What happens at each point between 9:00 AM and 1:30 PM?
- When is snack? When is outdoor or gross-motor play?
- When does Qur'an sit in the day?
- Is there a rest or quiet period?

## 6. Settling in — the first two weeks

At 2 years 10 months, separation anxiety is the parent's first fear. Nobody else
in Sarjapura is answering this well.

- Is there a staggered start, or shorter days at first?
- Can a parent stay in the room? For how long?
- What do you do with a child who cries at drop-off on day four?
- Who tells the parent how the day went, and how?

## 7. How families hear from the school

- Is there a WhatsApp group, a diary, an app, or a call?
- How often does a parent get an update on their own child?
- Who does a parent contact with a concern, and how fast do you reply?
- What happens at a PTM, and what does a parent take away from it?

## 8. Food, allergies and sick days

The FAQ currently says only "children bring their own food from home".

- Is there anything children may not bring (nuts, chocolate, fizzy drinks)?
- How is a known allergy recorded and handled at snack time?
- What is the policy on sending a child with a fever, cough or rash?
- Who is called first if a child falls ill at school, and what happens next?

---

## Two things needing a decision, not an answer

**Mission and vision.** Both have been rewritten to state what the school
actually does rather than how it feels about children. They need reading and
signing off before the client review — the words are currently ours, not the
school's.

**Parent testimonials.** Deliberately not on the site. A quote we wrote
ourselves is worth nothing, and inventing one is out of the question. In its
place the home page now carries a "why families trust us" section built from
facts a parent can independently verify — the Anjuman Taleemul Qur'an
affiliation, the Oxford curriculum, the single entry point, and the unannounced
walk-in policy. If the school can obtain two or three genuine parent quotes
**with written consent**, they are worth adding; without consent they are not.
