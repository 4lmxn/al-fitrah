# Project Brief: Al Fitrah Islamic Pre-School Website ## 1. Project Overview Al Fitrah Islamic Pre-School is a specialized educational institution in Sompura, Sarjapura, offering a 3-year integrated program that blends modern academic standards with deep Islamic values. The primary goal of this website is to drive admissions for the 2026-27 academic year by showcasing the school's unique curriculum, nurturing environment, and spiritual grounding. ## 2. Target Audience - **Primary**: Parents of children aged 2.10 to 3.10 years living in the Sompura/Sarjapura area. - **Secondary**: Local community members, prospective staff, and educational partners. - **User Needs**: Clarity on curriculum (especially Quranic studies), trust in safety and environment, ease of enrollment, and practical day-to-day resources. ## 3. Brand Identity: "Sacred Growth" - **Logo**: Stylized green sprout emerging from an open book with a golden crescent moon. - **Color Palette**: - Emerald Green (#065f46): Representing life, growth, and Islamic tradition. - Gold: Representing excellence and spiritual value. - Off-White/Cream (#faf9f4): For a clean, calm, and nurturing background. - **Typography**: Playfair Display (Serif) for headlines to convey tradition and prestige; Sans-serif for body text to ensure readability. - **Imagery**: Warm, brightly lit classrooms, children engaged in holistic learning (spiritual and academic), and soft geometric patterns. ## 4. Site Architecture (7 Screens) 1. **Home**: High-impact landing page focused on admissions, unique value propositions (Khatmul Qur’an), and campus highlights. 2. **Our Programs**: Detailed breakdown of the 3-year integrated curriculum (Arabic, Tajweed, English, Mathematics, Hadith). 3. **Campus Life**: Visual gallery and "Daily Rhythm" schedule showing the student experience. 4. **About Us**: Mission statement, educator certifications (Noor-el-bayan), and location/contact details. 5. **Admissions**: Step-by-step enrollment guide and lead-generation inquiry form. 6. **Parent Resources**: Hub for current parents (Academic Calendar, Lunch Menu, Uniform Guidelines). 7. **FAQ**: Categorized answers to common questions about pedagogy, fees, and Islamic integration. ## 5. Functional Requirements - **Lead Generation**: Inquiry form on the Admissions page integrated with school contact info. - **Responsive Design**: Optimized for Desktop and Mobile (essential for busy parents on the go). - **SEO Strategy**: Keywords targeting "Islamic Pre-school Sarjapura," "Montessori Sompura," and "Integrated Islamic Curriculum." - **Accessibility**: High contrast text and clear navigation labels. ## 6. Key Differentiators to Highlight - **Khatmul Qur’an Goal**: Recitation and memorization of 2 Juz by the end of 3 years. - **Noor-el-bayan Certification**: Specialized Arabic and Quranic instruction quality. - **Holistic Environment**: Focus on both "Deen and Dunya" (Spirituality and Worldly knowledge).
