## 3. How the split relates to the work

Worth setting out, because the instalments are not proportional to the hours behind
them — and the difference runs in the school's favour on the payment it makes first.

Of the 146 hours quoted, **80 are already built and running** — 55% of the project. At
the project rate of ~₹308 per hour, that delivered work is worth about **₹24,650**.

The first invoice is ₹18,000. The remaining **₹6,650** of already-delivered work is
carried into the two later stages:

| Payment | Hours behind it | Worth at project rate | Invoiced |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 — on signing | 80 | ₹24,650 | ₹18,000 |
| 2 — Stage 2 | 34 | ₹10,480 | ₹14,000 |
| 3 — handover | 32 | ₹9,860 | ₹13,000 |
| **Total** | **146** | **₹45,000** | **₹45,000** |

So on signing the school pays ₹18,000 for work worth ₹24,650 that it can already see
and use. The balance is released only as the remaining features are delivered and
accepted, and **₹13,000 — nearly a third of the contract — is not payable until
handover is complete**, including data import, training and transfer of the source code.

## 4. Acceptance

So that "accepted" is not something to argue about later:

1. Each stage is demonstrated to the school's point of contact, on the live system.
2. The school has **five working days** to raise anything that does not work as
   described in Section 3 of the proposal.
3. Anything raised in that window is fixed before the stage is accepted, at no extra
   charge.
4. If nothing is raised within five working days, the stage is treated as accepted and
   the invoice is issued.

Point 3 is not limited to the five days — the 60-day warranty in Section 10 of the
proposal continues to apply after handover.

## 5. Terms

- **Invoices** are issued by DVLN Solutions LLP and are payable within **15 days**.
  Bank details appear on each invoice.
- **GST** is charged in addition where applicable.
- **Running costs** — hosting, domain, payment gateway charges — are **not** part of
  this ₹45,000. They are billed by those providers directly to the school's own
  accounts. Estimates are in Section 6 of the proposal; at the current roll they come to
  roughly ₹1,400 per month, and the system can also continue to run at ₹0 on its present
  hosting.
- **Ownership.** On receipt of the final payment, the source code and all data belong to
  Al Fitrah Pre School, Sarjapura. Every third-party account is already in the school's
  name, so nothing depends on the developer to keep running.
- **Scope** is as listed in Section 3 of the proposal. Additions are quoted before they
  are built, at ₹500 per hour.
