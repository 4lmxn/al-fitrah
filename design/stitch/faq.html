Admissions Process

Our Montessori program is designed for children aged 3 to 6 years. We find that starting at age 3 allows children to fully benefit from the three-year cycle, establishing strong foundational skills in a nurturing environment before transitioning to the lower elementary grades.

Yes, we conduct a gentle assessment and a family interview. This is not a formal test, but rather an opportunity for us to understand your child's unique needs, learning style, and readiness. It also ensures that our educational philosophy aligns with your family's values.

Islamic Integration (Tajweed & Quran)

Students in the dedicated Hifz track spend approximately 2.5 to 3 hours daily on memorization, revision, and Tajweed practice. This is carefully balanced with their core academic subjects to ensure holistic development without overwhelming the student.

No prior knowledge is required for the early years or primary grades. We have a leveled Arabic curriculum that caters to beginners as well as advanced speakers. Our dedicated educators provide individualized support to help every child progress at their own pace.
